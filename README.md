# Blinkit-Sales-Analysis-Dashboard
# 🛒 Blinkit Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 1. Project Title / Headline

**Blinkit Sales Analysis Dashboard – Retail Performance & Outlet Insights**

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze Blinkit's sales performance, product categories, outlet locations, outlet sizes, and customer ratings to uncover meaningful retail business insights.

## 2. Short Description / Purpose

### 📌 About the Project

This project focuses on analyzing Blinkit sales data to understand sales trends, product performance, and outlet-level business performance.

**Project Objective:** To transform retail sales data into actionable insights that help identify top-performing product categories, evaluate outlet performance, and support data-driven business decisions.

## 3. Tech Stack

- **Power BI Desktop** – Interactive dashboard development and visualization
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – KPI calculations and measures
- **Microsoft Excel / CSV** – Data storage and preparation
- **Data Visualization** – KPI cards, donut charts, bar charts, area charts, and tables

## 4. Data Source

**Source: Kaggle**

The project uses the Blinkit grocery retail sales dataset available on Kaggle.

The dataset contains information about product sales, item categories, fat content, outlet types, outlet locations, outlet establishment years, item visibility, and customer ratings.

## 5. Features and Highlights

### 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard tracks four major metrics:

- **Total Sales:** $1.20M
- **Average Sales:** $141
- **Number of Items:** 8,523
- **Average Rating:** 3.9

### 📈 Outlet Establishment Trend

- Tracks outlet establishment trends from 2012 to 2022.
- Highlights changes in sales associated with outlet establishment years.
- Identifies 2018 as the highest point in the displayed trend, at approximately $205K.

### 🥗 Fat Content Analysis

- Compares sales of Low Fat and Regular products.
- Displays the contribution of each fat-content category to total sales.
- Low Fat products contribute approximately $776.32K, while Regular products contribute approximately $425.36K.

### 🛍️ Item Type Analysis

Compares sales across product categories, including:

- Fruits and Vegetables
- Snack Foods
- Household
- Frozen Foods
- Dairy
- Canned Goods
- Baking Goods
- Health and Hygiene
- Soft Drinks and other categories

Helps identify product categories that contribute most to overall sales.

### 🏪 Outlet Size Analysis

- Compares sales across Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 locations.
- Displays each location tier's contribution to total sales.
- Tier 3 locations generate the highest sales in the displayed breakdown, at approximately $472.13K.

### 📍 Outlet Location Analysis

Visualizes sales distribution across different outlet location tiers to compare regional performance and identify stronger-performing markets.

### 🏬 Outlet Type Performance

Compares outlet types using:

- Total Sales
- Number of Items
- Average Sales
- Average Ratings
- Item Visibility

The dashboard shows that Supermarket Type 1 contributes approximately $787.5K in sales, making it the leading outlet type by sales in the displayed data.

### 🎛️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes an outlet location filter to explore sales performance across different location segments.

### 📋 Comparative Visualizations

- KPI cards for quick performance monitoring
- Donut charts for category contribution analysis
- Bar charts for product and outlet comparisons
- Area/line chart for outlet establishment trends
- Summary table for outlet-level performance comparison

## 📌 Key Insights

- Total sales are approximately **$1.20 million** across 8,523 items.
- Low Fat products contribute a larger share of sales than Regular products.
- Tier 3 outlets lead the displayed outlet-location sales breakdown.
- Fruits and Vegetables and Snack Foods are among the top-performing item categories, each generating approximately $0.18M.
- Supermarket Type 1 is the largest contributor to sales among the displayed outlet types.
- The dashboard enables comparisons of sales, product categories, outlet characteristics, and customer ratings in one place.

It showcases how interactive visualizations can help businesses understand sales performance, compare outlet segments, and identify opportunities for improvement.
